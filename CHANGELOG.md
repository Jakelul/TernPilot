# Changelog

## 0.3.0-preview.1 — 2026-10-06

First public macOS preview of TernPilot, previously named DuoRelay.

- Route implementation to Codex GPT-6 Astra, strong design/review to Claude
  Opus, and eligible planning/feedback tasks to Gemini Pro.
- Handoff on Codex quota errors and bounded repeated failures.
- Stop on permission denial or cancellation without switching providers.
- Preserve handoff, decisions, history and execution logs in the repository.
- Offer a per-user Mac installer with optional new-chat Codex guidance.
- Cover routing, handoff, cancellation, state and installation with 20 tests.
- Provide a minimal devcontainer for cloud development and offline tests.

Source-available, all rights reserved; no open-source license is granted.
Windows, a background supervisor, pooled credits and universal interception
of app conversations are not supported.
